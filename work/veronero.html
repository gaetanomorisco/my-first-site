06 · Concept

Veronero Artisan Coffee

Year
2026
Type
Self-initiated concept
Role
Art direction, illustration
Made
Nine-post Instagram feed, launch posts

A seasonal launch for Veronero Artisan Coffee in Bari: four new autumn drinks, presented on the 18th of October.

The hand-drawn style comes from a steel poster behind their bar. I built a small illustration system around it, with a daisy mascot and the steel trays the drinks are served on, and a nine-post feed that alternates illustrations, the menu and a save-the-date.

Self-initiated concept, not commissioned by Veronero.
