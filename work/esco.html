05 · Concept

ESCO di giorno

Year
2026
Type
Self-initiated concept
Role
Art direction, design, illustration
Made
Two-sided menu, Instagram posters

ESCO is a listening bar in Bari that recently started serving coffee and pastries in the mornings. The concept treats the daytime offer as a record: coffee is Side A, pastries are Side B.

Working inside their existing look, with a pixelated serif, Helvetica and 1-bit images, I designed a two-sided menu laid out like an LP tracklist and a set of Instagram posters that sit naturally next to their real posts.

Self-initiated concept, not commissioned by ESCO.
