CON TEMPO Festival 2019

Year
2019
Type
Freelance, paid commission
Role
Sole designer
Made
Campaign concept, poster, billboard, street banners

A street campaign for the fifth edition of CON TEMPO, an art and music festival.

It was my first paid job as a freelancer. I designed it on my own, from concept to print: one black-and-white photograph and a tight typographic system, carried from the poster to the billboards and the banners that were printed and hung throughout the city.
