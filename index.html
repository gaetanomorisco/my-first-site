nice to meet you

I'm Gaetano, a graphic designer focused on posters, advertising campaigns and brand identities.

I started designing at 14 and never really stopped. I went on to study Product Design, which taught me to think in systems, to prototype, and to care about how things are made and used. I now bring that way of working back to graphic design.

Along the way I worked as assistant to the art director at ZN Consulting in Brussels on the identity of the EPP Congress 2018, interned at Eazycity in Cork, and designed my first freelance campaign, for CON TEMPO Festival 2019, printed and posted across the city.

Having lived in Ireland, Belgium, Denmark and Sweden, I'm used to working across languages and cultures. I'm currently open to design roles and freelance projects.
