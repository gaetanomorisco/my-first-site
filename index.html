Hello!
I'm Gaetano Morisco

Product designer by degree, graphic designer at heart — designing for passion since I was 14.

Freshly graduated in Product Design, I'm going back to my graphic design roots and setting myself new challenges along the way. I specialise in posters, advertising and branding, but I love design in every single one of its forms.

I've interned at Eazycity in Cork, Ireland, and at ZN Consulting in Belgium, where I worked on the EPP Congress in Helsinki — and designed the CON TEMPO Festival campaign as a solo project.
