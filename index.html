About

I'm a graphic designer working on posters, advertising campaigns and brand identities.

I've been designing since I was 14. I recently graduated in Product Design, which taught me to think in systems and to care about how things are made, and I now bring that back to graphic work.

I've worked with ZN Consulting in Brussels on the EPP Congress identity, with Eazycity in Cork, and independently for the CON TEMPO Festival.

Based in Bari, Italy. Previously lived in Ireland, Belgium, Denmark and Sweden.
